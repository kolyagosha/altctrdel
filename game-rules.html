How to play
ALT CTRL DEL · The card game of digital life
What you need
5–10 cards from the deck
2 or more humans
Drinks on the table
Phones face-down. Seriously.
Setup
1
Shuffle the cards. Place them face-down in the center of the table.
2
Everyone puts their phone face-down on the table. This is not optional.
3
The person who last checked their phone goes first. There's always one.
Each turn
A
Draw a card. Read the letter and poem out loud — with feeling.
B
Point at someone and say: "This is you."
If at least half the table agrees — they take the card.
C
If nobody agrees — you keep the card. The table has spoken.
D
Next person clockwise draws. Keep going until all cards are gone.
The one unbreakable rule
"If your phone lights up and you check it — draw two cards. No negotiations. No exceptions. The table decides what counts."
End of game
Who wins?
The person with fewest cards
Who loses?
Most cards = most roasted
What does the loser do?
Buys the next round. No debate.
Tie?
Both buy. The bar wins.
Two modes — pick one before you start
Chill mode
Most cards = most roasted = buys next round.
Laugh, drink, repeat.
Dare mode
Each card you hold = one dare from the table.
Loser still buys the round.
Sample dares (dare mode)
→ Tell us your most embarrassing phone moment. No filter.
→ Show the last 3 photos in your camera roll. Narrate them.
→ Give your phone to the person on your left for 10 minutes.
→ Say something true — out loud — about the person across from you.
→ Do 10 seconds of your best influencer impression.
→ Order another round. For everyone at the table.
